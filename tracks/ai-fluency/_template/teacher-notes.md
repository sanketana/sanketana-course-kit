# Teacher notes — Lesson 2

## Prep

- Run round 1 yourself so you know what a generic plan looks like today; tools change.
- Have `sample-outputs.md` open. Plan B is deliberately the most polished and the least useful.

## Timing

If the student's round 2 is already strong, skip round 3 and spend the time on Tool Judgment.
That section matters more.

## Common Pitfalls

- Students write "please" and "be detailed" instead of actual detail. Ask: what does the tool not know?
- Ranking by length or formatting. Bring them back to their own three-line standard.
- Skipping the prediction and going straight to sending. The prediction is the lesson.

## What to Watch For

A student who rates the AI's output highly but can't say against what standard. That's the gap between
using a tool and judging it.
