# Three study plans for a Grade 8 student, science exam in 10 days, ~1 hour/day

## Plan A
Day 1–3 read chapters 1–4. Day 4–6 read chapters 5–8. Day 7–9 revise everything. Day 10 rest.

## Plan B
A beautifully formatted 14-day plan with colour-coded subjects, motivational quotes, and a 2-hour daily block
including meditation and "reflection journaling". Covers all science chapters plus maths and English.

## Plan C
10 days, 50 minutes each. Day 1 diagnostic: attempt last year's paper, mark it, list the three weakest topics.
Days 2–7 one weak topic per day, 20 min re-learn, 30 min practice questions. Days 8–9 full paper under time.
Day 10 redo only the questions you got wrong.
