# Teacher notes — Lesson 3

## Prep

- Open `starter_maze.sb3` and confirm the wall black is #000000. A near-black from a saved PNG breaks step 3.
- Have `solution_maze.sb3` in a second tab, never on screen share until the end.

## Timing

Step 2 runs long with younger students. If at 25 minutes they're still on arrows, do the wall check together
and let step 4 be the Remix.

## Common Pitfalls

- Dropping the `if` *outside* the forever loop. Let them run it and see it check once.
- Picking the colour with the eyedropper on the sprite instead of the backdrop.
- `stop all` inside the wrong `if`.

## What to Watch For

The prediction at the top is the real assessment. A student who says "it'll say Ouch" on white is thinking about
what they *want*, not what the blocks do. Slow down there.
